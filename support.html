Sending feedback from the app

In the app, open Household and tap Send feedback. It starts an email with your app version, iOS version and device model filled in, which helps us find problems faster. You see everything before you send it.

If you joined the beta through TestFlight, you can also take a screenshot and share it with TestFlight's feedback option.

Sharing with another adult

One adult sets up the household. In Household, tap Invite another adult and send the link. The other adult opens it on their iPhone and joins with their own Apple ID, so you both see the same plan, grocery list and cooked dinners. Both phones need to be signed in to iCloud.

Each adult chooses Who sees my numbers. With Only me, your measurements, goals and numbers stay off the other adult's phone.

Kids' plates

Children's plates show hand portions, never calories or weights, and FamPrep never sets a weight-loss goal for anyone under 18. Parents can see the full numbers privately.

Resetting or deleting your data

  • Household → Reset FamPrep erases everything on your phone and in iCloud, for everyone you share with.
  • If you joined someone else's household, Leave household removes it from your phone only.
  • Deleting the app deletes the data on that phone.

FamPrep has no accounts or servers of its own, so there is nothing else to delete. Details are in our privacy policy.
