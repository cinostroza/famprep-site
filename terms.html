FamPrep estimates calorie and nutrient targets from the information you enter and from published federal and pediatric nutrition guidance. It calculates each plate's nutrition from USDA data and the recipe's amounts. Household → About lists the sources.

These numbers are general estimates, not a personal assessment. People's needs vary, and real nutrition varies with the brands you buy, how you cook and how you measure. Hand portions are approximate.

FamPrep isn't medical advice and isn't a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition. It doesn't replace a doctor, registered dietitian or other health professional, and using it doesn't create a professional relationship with anyone.

Talk to a doctor before you change how you or your family eat. This matters most if anyone in your household:

  • is pregnant or breastfeeding;
  • has a medical condition such as diabetes, kidney disease, heart disease or high blood pressure;
  • takes medicine that affects appetite, blood sugar or weight, such as insulin or a GLP-1 medicine;
  • has or has had an eating disorder;
  • is a child whose growth or weight concerns you or their doctor; or
  • has a food allergy, celiac disease or another condition that limits what they can eat.

Goals such as losing or gaining weight are each adult's own choice, and they aren't right for everyone. FamPrep plans children's plates for healthy growth, using pediatric guidance.

Stop following a plan and get medical advice if anyone feels unwell on it. Never ignore or delay professional advice because of something in FamPrep. If you think someone is having an allergic reaction or another medical emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number right away.

5. Allergies and food restrictions

How the filter works. When you set an allergy or restriction for someone, FamPrep won't plan recipes that need an ingredient tagged with it. If such an ingredient is only in an optional part of a recipe, such as a topping or garnish, FamPrep may still plan the recipe and leave that part off that person's plate. That food will still be in your kitchen and on other plates, so take care to avoid cross-contact, or mark the recipe Not for us in Recipes so FamPrep doesn't suggest it.

What the filter can't know. The filter works on FamPrep's own list of generic ingredients, such as "chicken broth" or "tortillas", not on the products you buy. Brands differ, manufacturers change their recipes, and a product can contain allergens that its name doesn't suggest, or come into contact with them where it's made. Our ingredient data can also contain mistakes.

What you need to do:

  • Add every allergy and restriction for every person, and keep them current.
  • Read the label of every product you buy and serve, every time.
  • Prepare, cook and serve food in a way that avoids cross-contact.
  • Follow the advice of the person's doctor or allergist.

FamPrep must not be the only safeguard for anyone with a severe food allergy, celiac disease or any other condition where a mistake could cause serious harm. Dislikes are preferences, not safety filters. If you find a mistake in our ingredient data, please tell us at support@famprep.app.

6. Cooking and food safety

  • Recipes, steps, times, temperatures, yields, cooked weights and leftover suggestions are guidance. Results vary with your stove, pans and ingredients, and new recipes may not have been tested in many kitchens yet.
  • Cook meat, poultry, seafood and eggs to a safe internal temperature, checked with a food thermometer. FamPrep shows USDA's safe temperatures for many dishes; for anything else, see FoodSafety.gov.
  • Follow safe food handling: wash your hands and surfaces, keep raw meat away from ready-to-eat food, and chill leftovers promptly. When in doubt, throw it out.
  • Cooking involves knives, heat, hot surfaces and appliances. Take care, follow your equipment's instructions and supervise children in the kitchen.
  • Recipe photos are illustrations. Your dinner may look different.

You're responsible for how you buy, store, prepare and serve food.
