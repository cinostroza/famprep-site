FamPrep Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Applies to the FamPrep iOS app (beta)

FamPrep plans family dinners, builds a grocery list, and shows each person what to put on their plate. We designed it so your family's information stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

What FamPrep stores, and where

FamPrep stores everything on your device, and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud database so your household can sync:

  • Household profile: names, birth dates, sex, height, weight, activity level, goals, training days, allergies, dislikes, and favorite recipes.
  • Plans: weekly meal plans, plates and grocery lists.
  • Your input: after-dinner ratings and cook logs (cooked weights, cooking times, notes).

We don't run servers that receive this information. iCloud data is stored by Apple in your account, where we can't read it. FamPrep has no accounts of its own: when you invite another adult with Household → Sharing, they join with their own Apple ID and see the household, plans and grocery list. An adult who sets Who sees my numbers to Only me keeps their numbers, measurements and goals hidden on the other adult's phone.

Household → Reset FamPrep erases everything on your phone and in iCloud (for everyone you share with). If you joined someone else's household, Leave household removes it from your phone only. Deleting the app deletes the data on that phone.

FamPrep uses no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and it doesn't sell or share data with anyone.

Children

Profiles for children are created and managed by a parent. FamPrep never shows calories or weight on a child's plating card, never sets weight-loss goals for anyone under 18, and never suggests supplements. A child's information stays on the parents' devices and in the household's iCloud like everything else.

Permissions FamPrep may ask for

  • Reminders: only when you tap the Reminders button, to add your grocery list to a list you choose.
  • Notifications: only when you start a cooking timer, to tell you when it's done.

Both are optional, and you can turn them off in iOS Settings at any time.

Things you choose to share

  • Share sheet: the grocery list is shared as plain text to the app you pick (Notes, Messages, …).
  • Cook log export: creates a file you choose where to send. It contains recipe names, weights, times and your notes, and no household or health information.
  • Send feedback: opens an email with the app version, iOS version and device model. You see and control everything before sending.

TestFlight

During the beta, Apple's TestFlight may share crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you submit through TestFlight with us. See Apple's privacy policy for how TestFlight handles data.

Health information

FamPrep uses federal and pediatric nutrition guidance. It is not a medical device and is not a substitute for a doctor or registered dietitian, especially for medical conditions or allergies.

Changes and contact

If FamPrep adds other features that send data off your device, we'll update this policy before that release.
