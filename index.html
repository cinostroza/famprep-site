The hard part isn't the recipe.

It's 5:30, and dinner has to work for four different people:

  • Youare cutting, and would like to log dinner without guessing.
  • Your partneris training, and needs more on the plate, not a different meal.
  • Your teenhas practice five days a week and is always starving.
  • Your youngestwould mostly like to know if there's pasta.

Family meal planners give everyone the same serving. Macro apps plan for one person. FamPrep cooks for the table and plates for the person.

How a week goes

  1. Sunday

    Plan the week

    3 min

    FamPrep suggests dinners that fit everyone. Swap the ones you're not in the mood for, keep the ones you are.

  2. Shopping day

    One list

    1 trip

    Every ingredient for the week, grouped by aisle and rounded to the packages stores actually sell. Send it to Reminders or any app.

  3. Weeknights

    Everyone's plate

    1 pot

    Cook once with short steps and timers. Then each person's card says exactly what goes on their plate.

For adults

Numbers you can log

Every amount comes from USDA nutrition data, never typed in by hand. Adults see grams, calories and macros for their own plate, ready for the tracker they already use.

For kids

Fed, not dieted

Kids' plates use hand portions: a palm of chicken, a fist of rice. No calories, no weights, no weight-loss goals for anyone under 18. Parents can see the numbers privately.
